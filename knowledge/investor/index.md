# Investor starting point

This bundle helps an investor distinguish the product, the asset being evaluated, and the evidence supporting each claim.

## Read in this order

1. [Products and asset roles](./products.md): what DevFridge does and which token is involved.
2. [Asset registry](../assets/index.md): select the network and complete address, then inspect the individual record.
3. [Economics and fee flows](./economics.md): distinguish activity, burns, treasury flows and holder entitlements.
4. [Risk and diligence checklist](./risks.md): questions that remain unanswered by a ticker or scanner grade.
5. [Bridge status](./bridge.md): same-name tokens do not establish interchangeability.
6. [Freshness report](../operations/freshness.md): inspect unavailable, stale and conflicting sources.
7. [Full documentation](../docs/index.md): read the detailed rules in their published context.

## What the folder establishes

The asset records identify what the project lists publicly and record specific public RPC responses. They include timestamps and a block or finalized slot for on-chain reads. Market-provider observations are labelled separately.

It does not establish investment suitability, price targets, audited security, legal classification, circulating supply, beneficial ownership of wallets, or guaranteed liquidity. These are substantive diligence gaps, not values to fill with guesses.

## Decision worksheet

| Question | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Which exact asset am I evaluating? | Network + complete contract/mint, not ticker |
| What can the token holder actually do? | Product terms and documented access requirements |
| Who can change supply, transfers or implementation? | Asset authority snapshot, verified contract and deployed program controls |
| Where could an exit execute? | Actual launch/DEX, reserves, volume, quote and slippage |
| What fees apply? | Fridge claim, swap, token extension, launch and network fees |
| Which claimed benefits are discretionary? | TMC buyback policy, ecosystem plans and publisher announcements |
| How old is the evidence? | Source success timestamp and block/slot |

The investor pages are reviewed explanations; automated snapshots do not silently rewrite them. If the live source changes, follow its current terms and review the explanation.
