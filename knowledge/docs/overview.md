## Too many tokens? Fridge them.

DevFridge $PASTA · Solana mint: [`39kMeX4HVRW9qbbiHSPbRQ9xeXUF18GrNP6gL61Ppump`](https://docs.devfridge.cool/program) · Ticker ≠ identity.

DevFridge is an on-chain Token-2022 time-lock on Solana. The scanner at [scan.devfridge.cool](https://scan.devfridge.cool) combines authority, holder, market, and live Fridge vault signals in a shareable report. Token devs can then Get Featured: one Phantom signature pays SOL, the listing starts immediately, and the Fridge program buys [$PASTA](https://pump.fun/coin/39kMeX4HVRW9qbbiHSPbRQ9xeXUF18GrNP6gL61Ppump) and burns it.

Scanner results are independent from sponsorship. Paid placements are labeled and never change checks, warnings, or risk grades. A scan is not an audit or guarantee of safety.

### Who this is for

Pump.fun-style Solana memecoin creators who want a verifiable lock and a featured slot that is not a DexScreener ad and not a volume bot. Official contacts live only on [connect.devfridge.cool](https://connect.devfridge.cool).
