DATA ON THIS DEVICE
Updated 28 September 2026

The app stores your collection, preferences and completed verified-score references on this device. Android backup for this app is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data and can remove registration references you still need. An unfinished run is not restored after Android kills the app.

The app does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera permission. The native wallet connection asks your wallet app to authorize connections and sign messages; recovery phrases and private keys stay in the wallet. The wallet may retain its own connection authorization. Disconnect or revoke it through the wallet as needed.

The age form sends the entered birth date to the DevFridge compliance service. Wallet addresses, timelock eligibility, self-exclusion choices, recorded moves and verified results are processed by online services. Clearing local storage does not erase those server records or cancel a self-exclusion.

Optional registration sends a short-lived handoff to the dedicated page in Phantom. On-chain score records, wallet links, token transfers and claims are public and cannot be erased by clearing this app. Other apps or services you choose have their own privacy practices.

For original-wallet approval, including Seed Vault, the service temporarily stores the exact score authorization and its Solana signature until the original challenge expires (at most five minutes). Phantom's browser also keeps the expiring challenge locally so it can resume after switching apps or tabs; it does not store private keys or signatures there. Clear Phantom's website data to remove that browser copy. Changing the payment account or fee token requires a new approval.

Sharing is initiated by you through Android's share chooser. A generated PNG is placed in app cache and temporarily granted to the app you select. Old share images more than 24 hours old are cleaned up when another image is shared, or when Android clears cache; cleanup is not a scheduled deletion service.

The optional SKR perk runs only after you tap Check balance and approve the notice. The selected public Solana address is sent directly to Solana's public mainnet RPC for a read-only balance request for the official SKR mint (SKRbvo6Gf7GondiT3BbTfuRDPqLWei4j2Qy2NPGZhW3). The RPC provider can see the address and your IP address. The app does not send a transaction or store the returned balance. A positive balance enables a cosmetic Aurora theme for the current game session only; it does not affect access, ranking or prizes. The public RPC is rate-limited. The adaptive run coach uses recent scores and your optional tip ratings only in local app storage; it does not send them to a model or server.

To manage device data, open Android Settings → Apps → DevFridge World → Storage. Clearing cache affects temporary files; clearing storage removes local history and settings. These are device controls, not a server-account deletion request.
