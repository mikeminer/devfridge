DevFridge

Games and tools for Solana. This is the publisher page of our apps for the Solana dApp Store.

Founder: Michele Angelo Forlani

The DevFridge collection is built around DevFridge's reusable Solana program primitives, such as the Fridge program, where anyone can lock a token until a date of their choice, and around $PASTA, the dev token. DevFridge Scan shows the locks, and the games below read them. Every app is free to play, and wallet features are optional. Documentation: docs.devfridge.cool.

Our apps

OnlyFridge Club

Android · online.onlyfridgeclub.app

A free 3D social nightclub for Android and the Solana Seeker. Dance, mix drinks at the bar, play billiards, bowling and arcade games, and sing karaoke live with the people in your room. The wallet is optional, with Seed Vault through Mobile Wallet Adapter.

App page Play in the browser Privacy Terms GitHub (guide and releases)

PIKKO

Android · art.pikko.game

A 3D healthy-eating game, about a minute a round: feed Pikko the healthier food and dodge the junk. It has 24 country food bags and 20 languages. Kid mode is on by default and hides every crypto screen. Free, with no account. The bags can optionally be unlocked with a DevFridge timelock, which the game only reads.

DevFridge World

Android · cool.devfridge.world

Cold Storage, the DevFridge 3D merge game: match the memes, discover the cast and keep the fridge door closed. The Android app bundles the game, with touch controls, haptics and Mobile Wallet Adapter sign-in.

MezzoPollo

Android · it.mezzopollo.academy

A 3D runner with a plush chicken that has lost half of itself: run through the gate with the matching half to complete numbers, words and shapes. It has 20 languages and an interactive tutorial. Play the Arcade's four paid levels, or the Subscription for holders of at least 10,000,000 actively timelocked MEMEZZO on DevFridge. The game only reads the timelock and never asks for a transaction to check it.
