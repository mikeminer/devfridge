# DevFridge Synapse

The viewer is adapted from [Magistra's public viewer](https://github.com/mikeminer/magistra/tree/347a8859aef179ab0553ae0cff206dc40a489dc7/public), licensed under GNU AGPL v3. The original license is included in LICENSE.

DevFridge changes: ecosystem branding, dedicated public knowledge feed, Solana and Robinhood groups, address search, a keyboard-accessible note list, freshness indicators, deep links, safe URL handling, responsive reading, and bundled dependencies.

Corresponding source, including these modifications: https://github.com/mikeminer/devfridge/tree/master/synapse

Three.js is MIT licensed. Lucide is ISC licensed, with portions under MIT. Their license files accompany the distributed libraries in vendor/.

The knowledge data remains sourced from https://github.com/mikeminer/devfridge/tree/master/knowledge. Viewer licensing does not replace the licenses of other repository components.
