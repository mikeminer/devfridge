## Reporting a vulnerability

Use only the contacts published at https://connect.devfridge.cool.

Please include:

- the affected program, API, frontend, bot, or SDK component;
- clear reproduction steps;
- the expected security impact;
- a safe proof of concept that does not expose user funds or secrets.

Do not open a public issue for an unpatched exploitable vulnerability. Never send a private key,
seed phrase, production secret, or unnecessary personal data.

## Audit status

No independent security audit is claimed or published at this time. Public source, automated tests,
and operational monitoring are transparency measures, not substitutes for an independent audit.

## Scanner boundary

DevFridge Scan reports observable on-chain and market signals. Its risk grade is not a security
audit, endorsement, investment recommendation, or guarantee. Sponsored placements never change
scanner checks, warnings, thresholds, or grades.
