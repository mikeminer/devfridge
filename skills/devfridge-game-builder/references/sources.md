- [MWA dapp identity verification](https://solana-mobile.github.io/mobile-wallet-adapter/spec/spec.html#dapp-identity-verification) and [Phantom domain and transaction warnings](https://docs.phantom.com/developer-powertools/domain-and-transaction-warnings): the wallet checks `identity.uri` against `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` and the release certificate. Checked 2026-10-25, Phantom's page links the domain-review form `https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1JgIxdmolgh_80xMfQKBKx9-QPC7LRdN6LHpFFW8BlKM/viewform`. Recheck that page before sending. The retired Blowfish form is not this path.
- [Blockaid contact](https://blockaid.io/contact): the public route for a false-positive or project-verification request. The live support-portal link can change; open the page and use the current one. No third-party paid whitelist is part of this gate.
- [Jupiter verification](https://verified.jup.ag) and [Express verification](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/tokens/verification): the free standard submission is the default. Express review costs 1000 JUP and needs a separate owner authorization. Verification is a review, not instant removal of a wallet warning.
- [Digital Asset Links](https://developers.google.com/digital-asset-links/v1/statements): `assetlinks.json` must be HTTPS, JSON, and match package plus signing-certificate fingerprint.

`devfridge.cool` as a blocked identity example, the several-day wait, the screenshot classes, Off as the submitted gore default, and the required APK string scan are lessons from a Store rejection. They are workflow gates in [wallet trust](wallet-trust.md), not new Solana Mobile policy text. No domain review, wallet signature or Store resubmission was performed for this package.
