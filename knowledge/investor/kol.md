# KOL product pitch and SDK pilot

DevFridge helps Solana Token-2022 communities, including compatible Pump.fun communities, turn developer and holder commitment into time-locked membership. Instead of relying only on a promise to hold, developers and community members lock tokens for a publicly verifiable period. Projects can offer access to services in exchange for a qualifying lock, using the SDK to verify membership. The dApp makes locking easy; Scan verifies the lock; badges, the bot and other products build on the same DevFridge vault.

The locked tokens cannot be sold from the vault before the unlock time under the program rules. This constrains the availability of that locked balance for the stated period; it does not freeze or guarantee price, market capitalization, all circulating supply or DEX liquidity. Access depends on the service’s configured membership rules, and Token-2022 mint/extension compatibility must be checked. Membership is not cost-free: applicable network, claim and token fees still apply. PASTA holding is not required to create a lock. SDK availability does not establish paying customers.

## What is available and what is proposed

The Fridge program, scanner, SDK reference, badge, bot and Synapse are published products. [World's current guide](../docs/world.md) describes a pre-launch merge puzzle with a 3D kitchen and an opening scheduled for 1 October 2026. Describe that documented build rather than calling it a live shooter. Bridge routes require separate network verification.

An SDK pilot is a proposed collaboration, not an existing signed partnership or a promise of token returns. Choose one concrete benefit: a research area, creator tools or a community resource. Confirm Token-2022 compatibility, custody/claim rules and extension support before asking anyone to lock tokens.

## Proposed 90-day pilot scorecard

- Days 1–30: define the benefit and success measures with one community, integrate and test access and renewal states.
- Days 31–60: measure first qualifying locks from voluntary participants, successful access and integration issues. Attribute outside participation only with evidence and consent.
- Days 61–90: report repeat use, renewal behavior and distinct participating wallets. A stretch goal of 40 independently attributed participant wallets is a proposal, not current traction or a guarantee.

Use the dated [protocol evidence](./protocol.md) as the baseline. The existing-account depositor count does not establish lifetime users, beneficial ownership or external adoption. Revenue requires transaction-level accounting; current burn value and the current boost-vault balance cannot substitute for it.
